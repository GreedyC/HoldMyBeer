# Teach a workflow with HoldMyBeer

Use HoldMyBeer as a local teaching aid. The current preview does not analyze recordings with AI. Read notes and answers as user-provided evidence, and keep uncertainty visible.

1. Ask the person to describe the task and the result they expect.
2. Invite them to record the selected browser tab or add notes. Recording is optional.
3. Ask focused questions where an important choice or checkpoint is unclear. Do not infer credentials, account state, or consent.
4. Help write editable procedure steps with a clear goal and an observable check.
5. Use the bundled-browser exercise to try a procedure with new fixture inputs. Report what the runner verified and what it did not.
6. Treat a failed or changed attempt as a prompt to ask the person for a correction. Do not silently promote a guessed repair.

Never claim AI video understanding, successful live social publishing, or automation of arbitrary websites. Do not ask for passwords or copy recordings outside the local preview.
